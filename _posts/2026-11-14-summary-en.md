### [Scaling 1T MoE on TorchTitan with FSDP+EP across 384 GH200 GPUs](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xvce5h/scaling_to_1t_moe_on_torchtitan_p/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A team scaled a ~1-trillion-parameter Mixture of Experts \(MoE\) model on TorchTitan across 384 NVIDIA GH200 GPUs, achieving 1,300 tokens/s/GPU — a 14× improvement over their initial baseline. They used a hybrid sharding strategy of Fully Sharded Data Parallelism \(FSDP\) across all 384 GPUs and 4-way Expert Parallelism \(EP\) confined within each node, avoiding Pipeline Parallelism \(PP\) due to its debugging and iteration overhead. Key technical enablers included full activation checkpointing with Grace-CPU offloading, Muon optimizer with FlexShard for local Newton-Schulz iterations, attention-cost-aware sequence packing, FP8 expert all-gather with blockwise scaling, and fixing CUDA stream–specific memory fragmentation via FSDP2’s unshard\_async\_op flag.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/f14-bertolotti · Nov 13, 16:28

**「Context」** TorchTitan is an open-source, PyTorch-native framework for large-model training, designed to simplify distributed training abstractions and integrate tightly with PyTorch’s ecosystem. The GH200 GPU — part of NVIDIA’s Grace Hopper Superchip — combines an Arm-based Grace CPU and Hopper GPU connected by a 900 GB/s C2C link, enabling novel host-offload strategies not possible on GPU-only systems.

**「Consequence for users」** Practitioners training trillion-parameter MoE models on multi-node GPU clusters can now adopt the documented FSDP+EP configuration, memory-aware checkpointing, and stream-local memory fixes to achieve &gt;10× throughput gains without loss in training stability — but must avoid Pipeline Parallelism if rapid iteration and failure diagnosis are priorities.
